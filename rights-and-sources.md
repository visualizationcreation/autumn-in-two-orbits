# Rights and sources

Six complete public-domain English-language poems, each from a named early edition or a source explicitly identifying the poem as public domain. No modern protected poem, translation, annotation or recording is reproduced. Typographic apostrophes may be normalized; wording and line order are preserved.

The selected authors died between 1821 and 1906; all selected underlying poems and consulted print editions were published before 1931. The public-domain statement here refers to the United States. Source pages retain their own terms for site content; this anthology reproduces the underlying poems, not the sources’ protected commentary or recordings.

The early Dickinson text is editorially titled and punctuated, and is not presented as a manuscript-faithful modern critical text.

## John Keats — To Autumn

Text as presented by the Academy of American Poets; written September 19, 1819, first published 1820. Three stanzas of eleven lines.

Text: https://poets.org/poem/autumn

Related artwork, linked only: https://www.artic.edu/artworks/14624

## Emily Dickinson — Autumn

Early published text: Poems (1890), “Nature,” XXVIII, editorially titled “Autumn”; reproduced from the collected early-series transcription. This is not the modern Franklin reading text.

Text: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/12242

Related artwork, linked only: https://www.artic.edu/artworks/127644

## Paul Laurence Dunbar — Merry Autumn

Lyrics of Lowly Life (1896), as presented and identified as public domain by the Academy of American Poets. Ten quatrains.

Text: https://poets.org/poem/merry-autumn/print

Related artwork, linked only: https://www.artic.edu/artworks/65845

## Helen Hunt Jackson — October

“October” from A Calendar of Sonnets. The consulted 1891 printing bears an 1886 copyright date; this is not asserted to be the poem’s first periodical appearance.

Text: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/9825/9825-h/9825-h.htm

Related artwork, linked only: https://www.artic.edu/artworks/876

## Robert Louis Stevenson — Autumn Fires

From A Child’s Garden of Verses (first published 1885), using the text of the 1905 illustrated edition. Three quatrains; illustrations from that edition are not reproduced here.

Text: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/25609

Related artwork, linked only: https://www.artic.edu/artworks/20827

## William Blake — To Autumn

From Poetical Sketches (1783); text as presented by the Academy of American Poets. Three six-line stanzas. Apostrophe shapes are typographically normalized.

Text: https://poets.org/poem/autumn-1

Related artwork, linked only: https://www.artic.edu/artworks/64736

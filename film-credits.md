# Autumn in the Pacific Northwest — film credits

Two separate ORB Feel & Experience film adaptations, English and Spanish. These recordings match the film transcripts linked below; the longer guided reading on the ORB remains a separate, unrecorded text edition.

The photographs are real. The connecting walk is imagined. The poets are not presented as Pacific Northwest writers. No generated imagery is used.

## Poetry and narration

Public-domain English editions: John Keats, Emily Dickinson, Paul Laurence Dunbar, Helen Hunt Jackson, Robert Louis Stevenson, and William Blake. The film includes selected passages; Dickinson’s eight-line poem is complete. All six complete English poems and historical notes remain in the ORB. The Spanish verse translations are newly authored for this adaptation, not copied from protected modern translations.

Original guide prose and Spanish adaptations authored with OpenAI assistance. Speech synthesized locally with Kokoro: Bella (af_bella) in English and Dora (ef_dora) in Spanish. These are synthetic guide voices, not recordings or imitations of the poets. Captions follow the language-specific recordings and measured timing.
